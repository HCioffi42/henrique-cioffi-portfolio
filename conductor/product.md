# Product Definition

## Vision
A personal website to showcase projects, blog posts, and professional information.

## Target Audience
- Recruiters and potential employers
- Other developers interested in my projects
- Readers of my technical blog

## Core Features
- **Blog:** List, view, and manage articles.
- **Project Showcase:** Display personal projects with descriptions and links.
- **Contact Info:** Provide ways to reach out.
- **Admin Dashboard:** Interface for managing blog posts and project details (if applicable).
